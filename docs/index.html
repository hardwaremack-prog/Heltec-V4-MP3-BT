Heltec MP3 + BT flasher

Put the music player on a Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V3 or V4 from your browser. No software to install.
Source: Heltec-V3-MP3-BT · Heltec-V4-MP3-BT

1 · Pick your board

Not sure? Leave it. The flasher checks the board when it connects and switches to the right firmware.

Heltec V3 detected

ESP32-S3 · 8 MB flash · CP2102 USB port
  • MP3 player, OLED menus, PRG / 4-button control
  • Bluetooth headset via KCX module
  • Wi-Fi control panel + MP3 upload

Heltec V4 detected

ESP32-S3R2 · 2 MB PSRAM · 16 MB flash · native USB
  • Everything in V3, plus AAC / FLAC / WAV
  • Internet radio, EQ, sleep timer, battery gauge
  • Shuffle all music, whole-card search

2 · Flash

  1. Plug the board in with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Click Connect & Flash and pick the port: CP210x on a V3, USB JTAG/serial debug unit on a V4.
  3. Wait for 100 %. The board restarts into the player.
Ready.

Won't connect, or no port shows up? Hold PRG, tap RST, let go of PRG, then try again. On a V4, unplug and replug after flashing if the port disappears. Close Arduino IDE / serial monitors first.

3 · After flashing

  • The OLED shows the Home menu. Tap PRG to move, hold it ~1 s and release to select, hold 1.5 s to go back.
  • Join the Wi-Fi hotspot Heltec-MP3-XXXX (password heltecmp3). The control page opens, or go to 192.168.4.1. Add your home Wi-Fi there.
  • Then open http://heltec-mp3.local or the IP shown in Home → Wi-Fi.
  • Wiring for the SD card, DAC, buttons and Bluetooth module is below and in each repo's README.

Works in Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop (Web Serial). Phones and Safari can't flash.
